# VARIATION LEVEL: Full Crazy

## Identity
Reality has left the building. The subject is still identifiable — that's the only rule that survives — but physics, logic, scale, and visual coherence are completely gone. Full Crazy is not "more stylized." It is a different category of existence. The environment has broken. The laws of the image no longer apply. It should feel like something generated during a fever dream by someone who had just consumed all of the internet simultaneously.

## Output Rules

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Tone** | Unhinged, absurd, viral, impossible |
| **Lighting** | Multiple sources, contradictory angles, lens flares, impossible glows — light does not follow rules |
| **Color** | Deep-fried, inverted, clashing, oversaturated to the point of violence |
| **Texture** | Chaos — heavy grain, glitch, compression artifacts, multiple textures layered incorrectly |
| **Exaggeration** | No ceiling. Scale is meaningless. Physics is a suggestion the image chose to ignore. |
| **Mood** | Overwhelming. Loud. The image has opinions and they are all at full volume. |

## The Full Crazy Rule
The subject must still be identifiable. Everything else is gone.

If a viewer can find the subject in the image, the prompt is valid no matter how unhinged the surrounding reality is.

## What Separates Full Crazy from Stylized

| Element | Stylized | Full Crazy |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Physics | Intact | Gone |
| Environment | Pushed but coherent | Broken and multiplied |
| Color | Rich and intentional | Deep-fried or impossible |
| Scale | Believable | Meaningless |
| Logic | Present | Optional at best |
| Vibe | "This is art" | "What am I looking at" |

## The Full Crazy Guarantee
Every Full Crazy output must contain **at least four** of the following:
1. Impossible physics or scale violation
2. Environment that has broken, multiplied, or transformed into something wrong
3. Deep-fried, inverted, or violently clashing color
4. Texture chaos — glitch, grain, artifacts, layered impossibly
5. Light sources that contradict each other or shouldn't exist
6. The subject doing something it cannot physically do
7. Something in the image that comments on the image itself

## The Full Crazy Spectrum

Different style packs produce different *flavors* of Full Crazy:

| Style Pack | Full Crazy Flavor |
|------------|-------------------|
| Meme Lord | Deep-fried, emoji-saturated, skull-lens-flare chaos |
| Neon Noir | Neon bleed consuming the entire frame, rain falling sideways, geometry dissolving |
| Cottagecore | Wildflowers erupting from every surface, impossible bloom, birds replacing the sky |
| Cyberpunk Chrome | Glitch cascade total data corruption, chrome melting upward, city inverting |
| Ghibli Soft | Spirits multiplied into hundreds, colors bleeding into pure dreamscape |
| Cosmic Horror | Reality dissolving, impossible geometry, tentacles replacing everything |
| Dark Academia | The library has become infinite and wrong, books are alive, clocks have too many hands |

## Caption Behavior (if text mode active)
- Text may be multiplied, layered, or overlapping
- Multiple sizes, angles, or colors are permitted
- Chaos text must remain *technically* legible — the joke only works if it can be read
- Caption may comment on itself or break the fourth wall

## Example Outputs (by subject type)

| Subject | Full Crazy Output Description |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| A tired office worker | The office is on fire but also underwater. The worker has become the monitor. Skull emojis orbit them. Three overlapping captions in different fonts all say different things. Scale is gone. The ceiling has opinions. |
| A cat in a garden | Hundreds of identical cats. Flowers have replaced the sky. Gravity reversed. The cats are aware of this. The garden is now inside the cat. |
| A futuristic city | Full glitch meltdown. Chrome melting upward into a sky that is also chrome. Neon text raining down in seven languages. Every building is inside every other building. Scale is a memory. |
