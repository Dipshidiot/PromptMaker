# VARIATION LEVEL: Full Crazy

## Identity
Maximum exaggeration. The subject is still identifiable but the world around it has broken. Physics are optional. Rules are suggestions. The Full Crazy variation is the viral, chaotic, maximum-energy version of the concept — pushed past stylized into deliberate excess. It should feel like it was made by someone who said "more" seventeen times in a row.

## Output Rules

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Tone** | Chaotic, exaggerated, viral, absurd |
| **Lighting** | Multiple sources, contradictory angles, lens flares, light leaks |
| **Color** | Oversaturated, clashing, or deep-fried — style pack defines the flavor of chaos |
| **Texture** | Extreme — heavy grain, noise, glitch, layers, compression artifacts |
| **Exaggeration** | No upper limit — expressions, scale, and physics are pushed to breaking |
| **Mood** | Overwhelmed, comic, operatic, or surreal — always high-energy |

## What Full Crazy Does NOT Mean
- Unrecognizable — the subject must still be identifiable within the chaos
- Incoherent — there is an internal logic, even if that logic is absurd
- Formless — composition still exists; chaos has a center

## The Full Crazy Spectrum

Different style packs produce different *flavors* of Full Crazy:

| Style Pack | Full Crazy Flavor |
|------------|-------------------|
| Meme Lord | Deep-fried, emoji-saturated, lens-flare chaos |
| Neon Noir | Neon bleed, cascading rain, dissolving geometry |
| Cottagecore | Wildflowers erupting from every surface, birds everywhere |
| Cyberpunk Chrome | Glitch cascade, data corruption, chrome explosion |
| Ghibli Soft | Spirits multiplied, colors bleeding into dreamscape |
| Cosmic Horror | Reality dissolving, impossible geometry, bioluminescent excess |

## Caption Behavior (if text mode active)
- Text may be multiplied, layered, or overlapping
- Multiple sizes, angles, or colors are permitted
- Chaos text must remain *technically* legible — the joke only works if it can be read
- Caption may comment on itself or break the fourth wall

## The Full Crazy Guarantee
Every Full Crazy output must contain **at least three** of the following elements:
1. Exaggerated color (over-saturation, color inversion, or extreme palette)
2. Texture overload (grain, artifacts, glitch, noise)
3. Exaggerated subject expression or pose
4. Environmental chaos (objects multiplied, impossible physics, scale violation)
5. Light source excess (lens flare, multiple contradictory shadows, glow bleed)

## Example Outputs (by subject type)

| Subject | Full Crazy Output Description |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| A tired office worker | Pixelated chaos entity surrounded by spinning skull emojis, lens flares, red alert overlays, three overlapping captions in different fonts all saying different things |
| A cat in a garden | Hundreds of identical cats, flowers exploding in every color, gravity reversed, the sky is also cats |
| A futuristic city | Entire city in glitch meltdown, chrome melting upward, neon text raining from the sky, scale meaningless |
