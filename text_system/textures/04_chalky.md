# TEXT TEXTURE: Chalky

## Identity
The specific texture of chalk on a blackboard, or pastel on paper. Soft, slightly dusty, with visible stroke direction and subtle variation in density. Chalky text feels handmade and immediate — like someone who knew what they wanted to say and just said it.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Surface quality** | Soft, slightly rough — uneven fill density |
| **Edge behavior** | Slightly frayed — edges are not crisp, chalk dust extends a little beyond |
| **Stroke texture** | Visible chalk stroke direction within letterforms |
| **Color rendering** | Uneven — lighter and darker areas within the same letter |
| **Dust effect** | Faint chalk dust haze around the text, especially at corners |

## Best Used With
- Dark Academia (chalk on blackboard — period appropriate)
- Cottagecore (handmade, tactile, warm)
- Retro Vintage (chalkboard menu aesthetic)
- Propaganda Parody (poster-draft feeling)
- Soft Wholesome styling (matches the handmade energy)

## Avoid With
- Cyberpunk Chrome (material contradiction — no chalk in the megacity)
- Luxury Brand (too rough for the precision aesthetic)
- Corporate Parody (professionalism has no chalk)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Light, careful chalk texture. Looks like a skilled chalkboard artist. Fill is mostly even with subtle variation. Edges are slightly soft. Dust haze is minimal. A beautiful, warm, handmade quality.

### Stylized
More expressive chalky energy — stroke direction visible and deliberate. Fill variation more pronounced. Chalk dust more visible at letter edges. Feels like the first pass on a large chalkboard mural.

### Full Crazy
The chalk has a life of its own. Letters are mid-smear. Dust clouds surround the text. Partial erasure marks are visible beneath. Some letters are heavy with multiple chalk passes; others are barely there. The board is running out of space and the writing is getting faster.

## Pairs Well With
