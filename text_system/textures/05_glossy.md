# TEXT TEXTURE: Glossy

## Identity
High-gloss lacquered finish. Text that looks like it has been printed, laminated, and polished. Clean specular highlights across the surface of the letterforms. The text doesn't just sit on the image — it commands it. Premium, polished, and confident.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Surface quality** | Smooth with clear specular highlights |
| **Highlight type** | Soft gradient specular — bright at the top of each letter, trailing off below |
| **Edge behavior** | Crisp, defined, slightly reflective at the edge |
| **Color rendering** | Rich and deep, with highlights creating dimensional depth |
| **Reflection** | Optional subtle reflection below the text |

## Best Used With
- Luxury Brand (native — lacquered product photography)
- Cyberpunk Chrome (chrome-glossy hybrid)
- Kawaii Pop (candy-gloss finish fits the aesthetic)
- Cinematic Epic (title-card energy)
- Corporate Parody (aggressively premium)

## Avoid With
- Grunge Street (gloss is the enemy of grit)
- Cottagecore (too manufactured for the pastoral)
- Dark Academia (the polish contradicts the wear)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Subtle, sophisticated gloss. A single clean specular highlight across the top of each letterform. Looks like high-end printing or a title card on a premium production. The gloss is felt more than seen.

### Stylized
Gloss pushed into design-visible territory — the highlight stripe is intentional and graphic. A slight reflection appears below the text. Color depth is rich. Feels like a polished product render.

### Full Crazy
The text is made of liquid glass. Highlights multiply. Reflections cascade. The text reflects the image it sits on. Light refracts through the edges. The letters are simultaneously transparent and opaque and both.

## Pairs Well With

| Text Styles | Placements |
|-------------|------------|
| Corporate Parody | Center, Bottom |
| Vintage Noir | Center |
| Soft Wholesome | Bottom (⚠️ use with intention — gloss can feel too manufactured for soft styles) |
| Bold Meme Block | Top, Bottom |
