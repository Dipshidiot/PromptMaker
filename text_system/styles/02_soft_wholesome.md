# TEXT STYLE: Soft Wholesome

## Identity
Gentle, warm, and human. Hand-lettering energy with rounded letterforms and a soft color palette. Feels like a note left on the kitchen table or a card from someone who actually means it. No aggression, no irony — just warmth.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Font family** | Rounded sans-serif (Nunito, Quicksand, Poppins) or casual script |
| **Weight** | Regular to Semi-bold |
| **Case** | Sentence case or lowercase preferred |
| **Color** | Warm cream, soft coral, dusty rose, sage green, or warm white |
| **Outline** | None, or a very faint soft shadow |
| **Letter spacing** | Slightly open |
| **Line spacing** | Generous (1.4–1.6) |
| **Alignment** | Center or left |

## When to Use
- Cottagecore (native match)
- Ghibli Soft
- Kawaii Pop
- Toy Box
- Any image with warmth, comfort, or gentle humor as the theme
- Affirmations, motivational content, celebration

## When NOT to Use
- Cosmic Horror (shatters the dread)
- Cyberpunk Chrome (too soft for the environment)
- Propaganda Parody (wrong power level)
- Grunge Street (tonally alien)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Clean, readable rounded font. Soft color — warm white or cream — placed thoughtfully in a clear area of the image. Generous spacing. Feels like it was placed by a person who cared.

### Stylized
Slightly hand-lettered quality introduced — subtle baseline variation, slightly inconsistent stroke weight. Optional small decorative elements alongside text (a tiny leaf, a small star, a soft dot). Color becomes a deliberate part of the design.

### Full Crazy
Text becomes a full illustration element — letters grow tiny flowers or leaves. Words may be in alternating soft colors. Small creatures or botanical motifs weave between letters. The text is still readable but it is also a tiny garden.

## Pairing Recommendations

| Best Textures | Best Placements |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Clean | Bottom |
| Chalky | Center |
| Grainy | Top |
