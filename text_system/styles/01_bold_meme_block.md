# TEXT STYLE: Bold Meme Block

## Identity
The dominant language of internet image macros. Thick, white, all-caps Impact-family typography with a heavy black outline. Immediately legible at any size, on any background. Built to survive a screenshot, a crop, and being saved seventeen times.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Font family** | Impact, Haettenschweiler, or any condensed ultra-bold sans-serif |
| **Weight** | Ultra-bold / Black (900) |
| **Case** | ALL CAPS always |
| **Color** | White fill |
| **Outline** | Heavy black stroke (2–4% of image width) |
| **Letter spacing** | Tight to normal |
| **Line spacing** | Tight (1.1–1.2) |
| **Alignment** | Center |

## When to Use
- Meme Lord style pack (native match)
- Any image intended for viral sharing
- Reaction images
- Humorous commentary on a visual
- When the text must be readable at thumbnail size

## When NOT to Use
- Luxury Brand (breaks the silence)
- Ghibli Soft (destroys the mood)
- Dark Academia (wrong century)
- Neon Noir (too crude for the tone)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Clean Impact font, white fill, standard black outline. Text sits at top and/or bottom with clear margin. No effects. Perfectly readable. The platonic ideal of a meme caption.

### Stylized
Slight warp or perspective on the text to follow the image geometry. Outline pushed thicker. Optional drop shadow added beneath the outline for extra depth. Still fully legible.

### Full Crazy
Text multiplied, overlapping, or layered in multiple sizes. Colors may invert (black fill, white outline) on some words for emphasis. Optional rainbow or gradient fill on individual words. Text may be at an angle. Chaos is legible.

## Pairing Recommendations

| Best Textures | Best Placements |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Clean | Top |
| Grainy | Bottom |
| Comic Halftone | Split (top + bottom) |
