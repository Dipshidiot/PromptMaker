# TEXT STYLE: Vintage Noir

## Identity
The typographic world of detective fiction, cinema posters, and jazz club handbills. Elegant, slightly worn, and heavy with atmosphere. Thin serifs, careful tracking, and a palette of cream, gold, and deep shadow tones. Text that sounds like a cigarette looks.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Font family** | Serif — condensed or display (Playfair Display, Bodoni, Clarendon, or a genuine period face) |
| **Weight** | Regular to Bold; ultra-bold used sparingly for key words only |
| **Case** | Title Case or ALL CAPS for headers; sentence case for body |
| **Color** | Cream white, aged gold, or pale amber on dark; or deep charcoal on light parchment |
| **Outline** | None — let the font do the work |
| **Letter spacing** | Wide tracking on caps (elegant); normal on mixed case |
| **Line spacing** | Generous — text breathes |
| **Alignment** | Center for headings; optional left for caption text |

## When to Use
- Neon Noir (native match)
- Dark Academia
- Retro Vintage
- Cinematic Epic (noir-variant)
- Any image with mystery, melancholy, or period atmosphere

## When NOT to Use
- Kawaii Pop (tonal catastrophe)
- Meme Lord (wrong era, wrong energy)
- Cyberpunk Chrome (fights with the aesthetic)
- Toy Box (too heavy for the mood)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Period-correct serif typography. Clean and elegant. Wide-tracked all-caps headers, generous leading. Cream or gold on dark. Feels like the opening title card of a classic film.

### Stylized
Pushed into graphic design territory — layered type at different scales, one large display word with smaller supporting text. Optional decorative rule lines or ornamental dividers. Feels like a genuine vintage poster layout.

### Full Crazy
Full pulp fiction cover energy. Multiple layers of type stacked at different angles. Distressed ink texture on the type itself. "The fog was thick... and so was the trouble." The typography IS the narrative.

## Pairing Recommendations

| Best Textures | Best Placements |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Clean | Center |
| Grainy | Bottom |
