# TEXT STYLE: Corporate Parody

## Identity
The language of corporate communication weaponized for humor. Clean sans-serif precision, professional confidence, and absolutely devastating irony. Looks like a LinkedIn post, a motivational poster in a breakroom, or a company all-hands slide — and means the opposite of what corporate text usually means.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Font family** | Clean corporate sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial, Inter, Gill Sans) |
| **Weight** | Regular for body; Bold for key terms and buzzwords |
| **Case** | Title Case for headings; sentence case for body text |
| **Color** | Corporate navy, confident charcoal, or crisp white; accent in brand blue or success green |
| **Outline** | Never — outlines are not professional |
| **Letter spacing** | Normal to slightly open — clean and readable |
| **Line spacing** | Normal (1.4) — efficient, not emotional |
| **Alignment** | Left-aligned (professional) or Center (for poster/cover formats) |

## When to Use
- Propaganda Parody (native match)
- Meme Lord (corporate variant)
- Any image satirizing work culture, professionalism, or institutional language
- Luxury Brand (dark corporate parody variant)

## When NOT to Use
- Cottagecore (no office in the forest)
- Ghibli Soft (no KPIs in the spirit world)
- Cosmic Horror (though… it could work)
- Kawaii Pop (cannot be made professional, will not)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Pixel-perfect corporate typography. Clean grid layout. Looks genuinely like internal company communications. The irony is entirely in the content, not the execution. This is the scariest version.

### Stylized
Pushed into the motivational poster format — large inspirational word at the top, clean supporting copy below, branded accent color, unnecessary achievement badge graphic. Feels like it should be framed in a lobby.

### Full Crazy
The corporate aesthetic has consumed everything. The image has a slide number in the corner. There is a pie chart. The buzzword density has reached critical mass. "SYNERGIZING CORE COMPETENCIES TOWARD A SCALABLE FUTURE OF IMPACTFUL DISRUPTION" in 48pt Bold over a photo of a dog.
