# TEXT STYLE: Chaotic Burst

## Identity
Maximum typographic energy. Multiple fonts, sizes, angles, and colors fighting for attention — and somehow it works. Rooted in zine culture, ransom-note aesthetics, and the visual chaos of someone who has too much to say and not enough time to organize it. Feels alive, urgent, and slightly unhinged.

## Visual Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Font family** | Mixed — minimum 2, maximum 5 different fonts in one composition |
| **Weight** | Mixed — ultra-bold for key words, light or regular for others |
| **Case** | Mixed — some words caps, some lowercase, no consistent rule |
| **Color** | 3–5 colors; high contrast; may include neon, black, white, and a wild card |
| **Outline** | Selective — only on some words, not all |
| **Letter spacing** | Inconsistent by design |
| **Line spacing** | Irregular — words stack, collide, or float |
| **Alignment** | None — deliberate misalignment is the rule |

## When to Use
- Meme Lord (high energy variant)
- Vaporwave Dream
- Grunge Street
- Kawaii Pop (soft chaos variant)
- Any content that benefits from energy, urgency, or maximum personality

## When NOT to Use
- Luxury Brand (fatal contradiction)
- Dark Academia (wrong chaos)
- Cinematic Epic (undermines the gravity)
- Ghibli Soft (too aggressive)

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Controlled chaos — two or three fonts, clear hierarchy despite the energy. Key words are big and bold; supporting words are smaller or lighter. Colors are used for emphasis, not randomness. Feels designed even if it looks spontaneous.

### Stylized
Full zine energy — cut-and-paste aesthetic, words at angles, drop shadows stacking, outlines on some letters only. Size variation extreme. At least one word is going a different direction from the others.

### Full Crazy
Ransom note singularity. Every word is a different font. Letters may overlap. Some words are upside down or rotated 90 degrees. Colors strobe across the composition. The text is doing a performance. You can still read the message but it takes a moment and that moment is part of the experience.

## Pairing Recommendations

| Best Textures | Best Placements |
