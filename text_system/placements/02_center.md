# TEXT PLACEMENT: Center

## Identity
Text occupies the visual and spatial center of the image. Bold, unavoidable, and compositionally dominant. The image exists around the text — not the other way around. A statement of intent.

## Positioning Rules

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Vertical anchor** | Optical center of image (approximately 45–50% from top) |
| **Horizontal position** | Center-aligned |
| **Text block width** | 70–90% of image width |
| **Background treatment** | Often requires overlay, blur, or darkening of image behind text |
| **Stacking (multi-line)** | Lines stack symmetrically above and below center point |

## Use Cases
- Single-word or short-phrase impact statements
- Affirmation or motivational content
- Title cards and cover images
- Luxury brand declarations
- Any time the text IS the image rather than a label on an image

## Style + Texture Compatibility

| Works Well | Use With Caution |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Vintage Noir (title card) | Chaotic Burst (may be too busy at center) |
| Soft Wholesome | Bold Meme Block (very aggressive at center) |
| Corporate Parody | — |
| Luxury Brand-compatible styles | — |

## Behavior Across Variation Levels

### Realistic
Text sits precisely at optical center. Image is treated behind the text — darkened, blurred, or color-shifted to create legibility. The balance between image and text is carefully considered. Neither overwhelms the other.

### Stylized
The image is designed around the centered text. Subject matter may frame the text from the sides or top/bottom. The text is given maximum visual weight. Graphic design sensibility dominates.

### Full Crazy
The text is the center of gravity for everything — the image literally orbits the centered text. Background elements point toward or radiate from the text. The text size approaches 30–40% of the frame. It is impossible to look away.

## Avoid When
- The subject is centered in the image (text will obscure the focal point)
- Using Split placement simultaneously
- The composition depends on an uninterrupted environment
