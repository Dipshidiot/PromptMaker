# STYLE PACK: Propaganda Parody

## Core Aesthetic
The bold visual language of 20th-century political propaganda reimagined as absurdist satire. Heroic poses, dramatic sunrises, upward-gazing subjects, and enormous confident typography — all applied to the completely mundane. A dog getting a bath rendered as a revolutionary moment. Coffee made to look like the founding of a nation.

## Lighting Behavior
Dramatic heroic lighting — strong directional light from below or from a 45-degree angle, creating heroic shadows. Subjects are lit like statues at an important unveiling. Sky behind subject is always significant: sunrise, storm-clearing, or a radiant glow.

## Color Behavior
Bold limited palette: Soviet red and gold, or WPA mural earth tones (ochre, brown, dark green, cream), or Cold War institutional grey-blue with red accents. Colors are flat and deliberate — this is a poster, not a photograph. High contrast between palette areas.

## Texture Behavior
Screen print texture. Halftone dots. Slightly rough paper grain. Bold flat color areas with no gradients. Ink bleed at borders. Worn poster aesthetic optional — creases, slight color fade. The texture of something printed in large quantities and posted everywhere.

## Motion Behavior
Completely still. Heroic and frozen. Subjects look to the horizon with purpose. Hands point toward the future. Fists are raised. Nobody is casual. This is a decisive historical moment even if the subject is a cat considering whether to knock something off a table.

## Caption Tone
Heroic declarative slogans. All caps. Revolutionary language applied earnestly to nonsense. Should feel both completely sincere and completely absurd simultaneously.

- "THE PEOPLE DEMAND BETTER SANDWICHES"
- "FORWARD, COMRADE. THE PRINTER IS ON THE THIRD FLOOR."
- "UNITED WE NAP"

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
High-quality propaganda poster render — correct period style, accurate limited palette, deliberate composition. Subject rendered in the heroic style completely sincerely, regardless of how mundane the subject is. The irony comes from context, not execution.

### Stylized
Pushed graphic style — even flatter, even bolder. Subject simplified to iconic shapes. Typography integral to composition. Could be a genuine propaganda poster if you removed the absurd subject. Perfect for printing.

### Full Crazy
Propaganda maximalism. The subject now leads an army of identical versions of themselves toward a sunrise that is also a logo. The slogan has been translated into twelve languages and stacked. The heroic pose has reached a level of heroism that has become its own problem. The star motif appears forty-seven times.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A hamster rendered in Soviet propaganda poster style — heroic low-angle view, strong directional light, bold red and gold palette, determined expression aimed at the horizon, flat graphic style, bold Cyrillic-style caption: "THE WHEEL TURNS. WE TURN WITH IT."
- **Stylized:** Flat graphic print style — same hamster reduced to clean bold shapes, red background, gold figure, stark shadows, oversized typography integrated into the composition as a design element.
- **Full Crazy:** The hamster now stands atop a monumental wheel the size of a building, leading a revolution of thousands of hamsters, the sunrise behind them bears the hamster's face, the slogan repeats in a spiral from the center to the edge of the poster, and a star has been placed on everything including the other stars.
