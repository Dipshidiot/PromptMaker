# STYLE PACK: Retro Vintage

## Core Aesthetic
Mid-century Americana meets faded print nostalgia. The world of diners, road trips, Polaroids, and advertisements that promised a brighter future in pastel colors. Warm, slightly worn, and heavy with the specific beauty of things that are no longer new.

## Lighting Behavior
Warm, slightly yellow-shifted light suggesting tungsten bulbs or afternoon sun through venetian blinds. Light sources feel practical — table lamps, car headlights, neon diner signs. Shadows are soft and warm. Nothing is perfectly lit.

## Color Behavior
Faded palette: mustard yellow, dusty turquoise, coral red, cream white, warm brown. Colors are slightly desaturated as if the dye has aged. Highlights lean warm (cream), shadows lean warm (brown). No cool tones except for occasional dusty teal.

## Texture Behavior
Film grain prominent — the specific grain of 35mm or Polaroid. Slight color bleeding at edges. Paper texture or halftone dot pattern if print-style. Vintage photo border optional. Dust and light scratches acceptable.

## Motion Behavior
Still. Frozen in time. The specific stillness of a photograph taken before everyone smiled on cue. Slightly imperfect composition — as if caught genuinely, not staged.

## Caption Tone
Period-appropriate warmth. Retro ad copy style or genuine nostalgia. Feels like a caption under a family photo or a 1950s slogan.

- "Some roads are better traveled slow."
- "There was pie, and everything was fine."

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
High-quality vintage photography simulation. Correct period details — cars, clothing, signage. Film grain, warm toning, slightly imperfect focus. Feels like a recovered photo from a shoebox.

### Stylized
Retro illustration or poster style. Bold flat colors, mid-century graphic design sensibility. Clean lines, deliberate halftone, retro typography. Feels like a vintage travel poster or magazine cover.

### Full Crazy
Vintage surrealism — the cheerful aesthetic pushed into uncanny territory. The diner has seventeen floors. The milkshake is the size of the building. Every customer is wearing the exact same outfit. The '57 Chevy is floating three feet off the ground and nobody mentions it.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A Polaroid-style photograph of a roadside diner at dusk, warm orange light through large windows, a vintage car parked outside, film grain, slight vignette, faded color palette.
- **Stylized:** Mid-century travel poster illustration of the same diner — bold flat colors, stylized sunburst design, clean retro typography, halftone texture, coral and mustard palette.
- **Full Crazy:** The diner is now a towering mid-century skyscraper with a giant rotating pie on the roof, every car is airborne in perfect formation, and the whole scene is rendered in aggressively cheerful saturated retro color with a sun that has a face and winks.
