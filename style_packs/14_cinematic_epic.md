# STYLE PACK: Cinematic Epic

## Core Aesthetic
The visual grammar of blockbuster cinema at its most grand. Wide vistas, heroic framing, practical scale, and the specific feeling that something of tremendous consequence is about to happen. Every frame is a poster. Every moment is the moment.

## Lighting Behavior
Golden hour as the default — warm, directional, rim-lit subjects against dramatic skies. Volumetric light rays through clouds or fog. Epic backlit silhouettes. Lens flares used meaningfully. The lighting tells you this matters.

## Color Behavior
Teal and orange color grade (the cinematic standard) — warm subjects against cool environments, or vice versa. High contrast with lifted blacks (filmic tone curve). Greens pushed teal. Skin tones warm and golden. Sky is often dramatic — churning, colorful, active.

## Texture Behavior
35mm film grain. Slight anamorphic lens distortion at edges. Dust particles in light beams. Atmospheric haze in distance. Real textures — worn leather, aged stone, weathered fabric — rendered with loving detail.

## Motion Behavior
Sweeping and deliberate. The camera would be moving in a slow, grand arc if this were a film. Subjects are still at the peak moment — the breath before action. Wind moves cloaks and hair. Flags and banners wave.

## Caption Tone
Declarative and weighty. Film title energy. Short phrases that feel like destiny.

- "Some journeys only end one way."
- "The last of their kind. The first of what comes next."

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Photo-real cinematic quality. Correct film grain, accurate color grade, real or photo-real environments. Feels like a frame from a major studio production. Subject is heroically lit and composed.

### Stylized
Concept art / matte painting style. Painted but photorealistic quality. Dramatic composition pushed further — subject smaller against a larger world, or close-up with environmental storytelling in background. Feels like official key art.

### Full Crazy
Impossible cinematic maximalism. The sky has three sunsets stacked. The army behind the hero is a million strong and they stretch to the horizon and beyond the horizon and also into the sky. Every landscape element is turned up to eleven. The lens flare occupies 40% of the frame and it is justified.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A lone warrior standing on a cliff edge at golden hour, storm clouds building behind them, warm rim light defining their silhouette, teal-and-orange grade, volumetric light rays through cloud breaks, cinematic 2.39:1 aspect ratio crop.
- **Stylized:** Concept art of the same warrior — painted but detailed, pushed color drama, vast landscape behind them emphasizing scale, armies visible in the valley below, epic caption in clean serif font.
- **Full Crazy:** The warrior stands at the junction of three impossibly colored sunsets, a tidal wave of golden light cresting behind them, the army below numbers in the absurd millions and they are all individually lit, lens flares from seventeen simultaneous sources, and the clouds have formed a face that is watching.
