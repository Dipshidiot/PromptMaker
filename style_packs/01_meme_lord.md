# STYLE PACK: Meme Lord

## Core Aesthetic
High-impact internet meme energy. Bold, instantly recognizable, unapologetically loud. Rooted in viral image formats, reaction culture, and maximum relatability. Everything is exaggerated for comedic effect. The visual language of the internet distilled into a single frame.

## Lighting Behavior
Flat, overexposed lighting. No dramatic shadows — everything is washed out and readable at a glance. Harsh flash-photography feel. Whites are blown out, faces are slightly shiny.

## Color Behavior
Saturated primaries: electric red, meme yellow, impact white, deep black. High contrast. No subtle gradients. Colors pop like a notification alert. Background often solid white, grey, or a single blown-out color.

## Texture Behavior
Slightly compressed JPEG artifact texture — the "saved 47 times" look. Slight pixelation on edges. White borders optional. No smooth rendering.

## Motion Behavior
Static and frozen. Subjects are caught mid-expression — peak reaction face. No blur. No movement. Just the decisive, absurd moment locked in time.

## Caption Tone
All caps. Impact font energy. Short, punchy, brutally relatable. Often two-part structure (top setup / bottom punchline). Self-aware, ironic, or earnest to the point of comedy.

- "WHEN YOU REALIZE IT'S ONLY TUESDAY"
- "ME: I'll sleep early. ALSO ME AT 3AM:"

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Photograph-quality image of a person, animal, or object caught in a genuinely funny or relatable moment. Natural setting. Slightly over-lit. Caption uses clean bold text in white with black outline.

### Stylized
Enhanced comic exaggeration — expressions are pushed 30% further. Colors are punched up. Compression artifacts become part of the aesthetic. Text is bold, slightly warped, with heavy shadow.

### Full Crazy
Full meme stack chaos. Multiple reaction faces layered. Deep-fried filter applied — extreme saturation, lens flares, emojis orbiting the subject. Caption is barely legible through the noise. Noise IS the message.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A tired office worker staring blankly at their monitor, flat fluorescent lighting, white background, Impact font caption: "ANOTHER MONDAY. ANOTHER MISSION IMPOSSIBLE."
- **Stylized:** Same worker with cartoonishly wide eyes, pupils replaced with spinning loading icons, saturated yellow wash, JPEG grain, caption warped and shadowed.
- **Full Crazy:** The worker is now a pixelated chaos god, surrounded by spinning 💀 emojis, lens flares, red alert overlays, and three overlapping captions in different fonts that all say different things simultaneously.
