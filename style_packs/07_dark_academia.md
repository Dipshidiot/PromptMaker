# STYLE PACK: Dark Academia

## Core Aesthetic
Gothic university halls, candlelit libraries, secret societies, and the romantic pursuit of dangerous knowledge. Autumn leaves, ink stains, leather-bound books, and the specific melancholy of knowing too much too young. Intellectual beauty with an undercurrent of dread.

## Lighting Behavior
Low-key candlelight and desk lamp warmth against deep shadows. Fireplace glow. Dusty afternoon light through tall leaded windows. The dominant feeling is shadow interrupted by small warm pools of light. Darkness is comfortable here, not threatening.

## Color Behavior
Deep mahogany, hunter green, aged parchment, burgundy, near-black, warm gold. Palette is rich and autumnal but dark. Desaturated mid-tones. Only warm light sources provide saturation; everything else is deep and muted.

## Texture Behavior
Aged paper and parchment. Leather grain. Wood with varnish cracks. Stone and mortar. Ink bleed on paper. Dust motes visible in shafts of light. Bookcloth and gilded lettering. Everything has been touched by many hands over many years.

## Motion Behavior
Very slow. Pages turn. Candle flames waver in imperceptible drafts. A pen moves across paper. Dust settles. Time is irrelevant here — only the text matters.

## Caption Tone
Literary, elevated, slightly melancholic. Quotes that feel like they belong underlined in a 19th-century novel. Dense with meaning.

- "We were the kind of students who read past midnight and called it living."
- "All the best secrets are kept in libraries."

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Cinematic photography of a real or real-looking academic environment. Rich practical lighting, deep focus on textures — spines of books, brass candlesticks, handwritten notes. Feels like a still from a prestige period drama.

### Stylized
Oil painting or detailed illustration style. Chiaroscuro lighting pushed further. Subject rendered with painterly attention to fabric and expression. Background is lush but dark — shelves receding into shadow.

### Full Crazy
The library has become infinite. Staircases spiral into darkness. Books open themselves. The candles are the only things that haven't moved in 200 years. A crow sits on the globe. The clock has too many hands. The secret society meeting is in progress and everyone is wearing something deeply concerning.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A student at a mahogany desk surrounded by stacked books, single desk lamp casting warm light, deep shadows, autumn leaves visible through a tall window, parchment and ink on the desk.
- **Stylized:** Oil painting rendition — same desk rendered in Rembrandt lighting, every book spine lettered in gold, the student's expression unreadable, shadows thick and velvety, the window showing a stormy autumn sky.
- **Full Crazy:** The desk is now the center of a vast impossible library with staircases defying gravity, every book open and floating, candles hovering mid-air, a raven on each shoulder, and ancient text spiraling through the air like smoke.
