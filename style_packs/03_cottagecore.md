# STYLE PACK: Cottagecore

## Core Aesthetic
Soft pastoral fantasy. The romantic ideal of rural life — wildflowers, warm bread, morning fog, wooden furniture, and linen everything. Gentle, nostalgic, and deeply comforting. An escape from modern noise into a world where the biggest event of the day is picking berries before the rain.

## Lighting Behavior
Soft golden hour and overcast diffused light. No harsh shadows. Warm morning glow filtering through lace curtains or forest canopy. Dappled light on foliage. Everything feels illuminated from within.

## Color Behavior
Muted earth tones: sage green, dusty rose, warm cream, soft brown, sky grey-blue, lavender. Desaturated and gentle — no bold or loud colors. Palette feels like watercolor left in sunlight.

## Texture Behavior
Linen, woven fabric, rough wood grain, pressed flowers, watercolor wash, hand-embroidery. Paper and parchment textures. Soft vignette edges. Everything has a tactile, handmade quality.

## Motion Behavior
Almost still. A gentle breeze moves curtains or tall grass. Smoke drifts slowly from a chimney. Leaves fall softly. Time moves slowly, peacefully.

## Caption Tone
Gentle, poetic, lowercase. Quotes about simple joys, nature, and warmth. Feels handwritten. No urgency.

- "flour on my hands and nowhere to be"
- "the kettle is on and the world is very small today"

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Soft-focus photography. Golden hour light on a real cottage garden or kitchen. Natural textures prominent. Feels like a styled photoshoot for a slow-living magazine.

### Stylized
Watercolor or gouache illustration style. Loose brushstrokes, warm tones, botanical details lovingly rendered. Slightly dreamlike — proportions can be gently exaggerated for whimsy.

### Full Crazy
Full fairy-tale maximalism. Wildflowers growing from every surface. Mushrooms the size of armchairs. Woodland creatures at the tea table. Vines cascading through windows. The cottage has definitely become sentient and is very cozy about it.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A wooden kitchen table with fresh-baked sourdough, a jar of wildflowers, warm morning light through a lace curtain, soft cream palette, film-quality texture.
- **Stylized:** Watercolor illustration of the same scene — loose flower brushstrokes, soft shadows, warm gouache color, handwritten caption in flowing lowercase.
- **Full Crazy:** The kitchen overflows with impossible flowers, a fox in an apron stirs a pot of glowing stew, the bread has tiny mushroom doors, and ivy frames every edge of the image in dense botanical chaos.
