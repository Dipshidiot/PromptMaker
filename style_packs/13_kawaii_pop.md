# STYLE PACK: Kawaii Pop

## Core Aesthetic
Japanese cute culture at maximum amplitude. Everything is soft, round, pastel, and expressive. Eyes are large. Cheeks are perpetually flushed. Objects have faces and feelings. The world is a safe place full of small joys and large desserts. Even sadness here is kind of adorable.

## Lighting Behavior
Soft diffused studio light from all directions — no hard shadows. Everything glows faintly from a pastel ambient light. Sparkle catch-lights in eyes. Small stars and heart shapes manifest as actual light sources.

## Color Behavior
Pastel everything: baby pink, lavender, mint green, peach, butter yellow, sky blue. White space is abundant. Occasional high-saturation accent in candy red or electric blue for emphasis. Palette is consistent and controlled — no dark or grey tones unless for intentional chibi-sad effect.

## Texture Behavior
Smooth and soft — mochi-like. Slight gradient on rounded forms suggesting softness. Glitter and sparkle as texture elements. Tiny stars, hearts, and flowers as decorative surface textures. Skin is smooth and slightly shiny. Food is glossy.

## Motion Behavior
Bouncy and springy. Subjects wobble slightly. Sparkles drift upward. Hearts float gently. Expressions shift rapidly and expressively. Everything moves like it has a personality and the personality is "very enthusiastic."

## Caption Tone
Enthusiastic, sweet, emoji-adjacent. Exclamation points are mandatory. Often describes emotions directly and sincerely.

- "today is a cake day!! ♡"
- "u r doing GREAT and also there are stars ✨"
- "softness loading... ██████ 100%"

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
High-quality kawaii illustration with clean digital painting technique. Proportions are stylized but consistent. Rich pastel palette, clean linework, expressive character design. Feels like premium sticker-pack art or a game character portrait.

### Stylized
Full chibi exaggeration — 2-head-tall proportions, massive eyes, tiny hands. Sparkle effects visible. Background is pattern-filled with tiny motifs. Caption in rounded bubbly font. Feels like official character merchandise.

### Full Crazy
Kawaii singularity. Every element has a face. The food has faces. The furniture has faces. The sparkles have faces. Subject is surrounded by a crowd of small creatures all reacting to them. The background is a pastel explosion of every kawaii motif ever created simultaneously. Someone is eating a cake the size of a building and it is very happy about it.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A round-faced character with large sparkly eyes holding a strawberry crepe, pastel pink background with subtle star pattern, soft blush on cheeks, clean digital illustration, small hearts floating upward.
- **Stylized:** Chibi version — same character now 2 heads tall, enormous glittery eyes, crepe has a tiny smiling face, background is dense with floating stars, hearts, and small animal friends, rounded bubbly font caption: "crepe day!! ♡"
- **Full Crazy:** The crepe is now a building-sized tower with a face and a crown. The character is riding it. Every food item in existence surrounds them, all with faces and emotions. The sky is made of macarons. The sparkles have formed a constellation spelling "SOFT MODE ACTIVATED."
