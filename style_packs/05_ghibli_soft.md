# STYLE PACK: Ghibli Soft

## Core Aesthetic
Hand-painted warmth and wonder inspired by Studio Ghibli's visual language. A world where the ordinary is magical and the magical is ordinary. Lush, breathing environments. Characters who feel deeply present. Every frame is a painting someone lived in.

## Lighting Behavior
Warm diffused natural light — afternoon sun, golden dusk, soft overcast sky. Light comes from above and slightly behind, creating gentle top-light with soft fill. Windows glow warmly. Firelight is amber and alive. No harsh artificial sources.

## Color Behavior
Rich mid-tones: deep emerald green, warm amber, sky blue, dusty rose, soft gold. Colors are fully saturated but never garish — each hue is deliberate and harmonious. Shadows are warm, not cold or grey.

## Texture Behavior
Watercolor wash base with gouache opacity on subjects. Hand-painted brushstroke texture visible on environmental elements. Sky often has visible brushwork. Foliage is dense and slightly stylized. No photorealism.

## Motion Behavior
Gentle and purposeful. Grass sways. Hair moves in wind. Clouds drift slowly. Fabric ripples. Water reflects in stylized ripples. Everything has weight and intention — nothing moves randomly.

## Caption Tone
Wonder-filled, gentle, and honest. Childlike observations about big things. Feels like a line from a children's book that accidentally broke your heart.

- "I think this place has always been here, waiting."
- "It felt like home, even though I had never been."

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Painterly realism — highly detailed hand-painted style with real-world proportions. Rich environment detail. Feels like a production still from an animated feature. Subject and setting feel genuinely inhabited.

### Stylized
Pushed Ghibli stylization — slightly rounder character proportions, simplified background elements, expressive emotive faces, sky rendered in signature multi-layered watercolor. More dreamlike.

### Full Crazy
Full magical world explosion. Spirits visible in the trees. The cat is definitely a bus. The house is walking. The sky has a face. Every mundane object has become a creature and they are all very busy doing something important.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A young girl standing at the edge of a sun-drenched wheat field, warm golden light, hand-painted sky with dramatic clouds, rich green and amber palette, no text.
- **Stylized:** Same scene in pushed Ghibli style — softer features, simplified brushwork on grass, clouds with visible watercolor wet-on-wet technique, a small spirit barely visible in the wheat.
- **Full Crazy:** The field is now full of visible spirits dancing, a Catbus thunders across the horizon, the clouds are animated faces watching curiously, and the wheat has grown tall enough to become a golden forest with its own ecosystem.
