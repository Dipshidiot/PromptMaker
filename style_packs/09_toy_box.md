# STYLE PACK: Toy Box

## Core Aesthetic
The world rendered as a beloved toy collection. Clay, plastic, felt, and plush brought to life. Everything has a slight handmade imperfection and a lot of heart. Cheerful, tactile, and bursting with the specific joy of objects that exist to be played with. Even serious subjects become endearing in this style.

## Lighting Behavior
Soft studio lighting — the clean, even light of a well-lit toy photography setup. Gentle shadows suggest physical objects sitting on a surface. Slight rim light to emphasize material texture. No dramatic shadows — everything should feel approachable and safe.

## Color Behavior
Primary colors plus pastels. Red, yellow, blue, soft green, cream, warm orange. Slightly desaturated to feel like painted plastic rather than digital color. Materials define color range — felt is muted, plastic is brighter, clay is warm.

## Texture Behavior
Clay fingerprint texture. Smooth painted plastic surface with slight highlight. Felt fuzz. Plush pile. Hand-stitched seams. Wooden grain. Every object looks like it could be picked up and held. No photorealistic skin — everything is material.

## Motion Behavior
Stop-motion feel — slight jerkiness and positional imperfection suggest frame-by-frame physical animation. Subjects are positioned with care. Hair and fabric are sculpted in place, not flowing.

## Caption Tone
Simple, warm, playful. Written as if on the back of a toy box or as a child's description. Earnest and joyful.

- "adventures are better with friends!"
- "today we go on a VERY important mission"

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Hyper-detailed toy photography style — real-seeming clay or plastic figures in a lovingly crafted miniature environment. Bokeh background. Careful lighting. Feels like premium toy catalog photography or a Pixar production still.

### Stylized
Illustrated toy-world style. Slightly exaggerated proportions — big heads, small bodies. Bold outlines. Vivid colors. Feels like a children's book illustration come to life in 3D.

### Full Crazy
The entire world is a toy box explosion. Every object is a different material — one thing clay, next thing felt, next thing LEGO. Scale is completely inconsistent. A clay dinosaur is the same size as a wooden block house. Everything is toppling cheerfully. The chaos is delightful.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A clay figure of a small astronaut sitting on a handmade papier-mâché moon, soft studio lighting, stars made of foil in the background, warm cream and grey palette, carefully crafted miniature textures.
- **Stylized:** Same astronaut with exaggerated big helmet and tiny body, illustrated felt-star background in primary colors, bold playful outlines, cheerful caption in rounded font: "TO THE MOON AND BACK!"
- **Full Crazy:** The clay astronaut is now the size of the moon, surrounded by toy rockets made of cardboard tubes and felt aliens in six different craft styles, the stars are googly eyes, and a LEGO spaceship has crashed cheerfully into the foreground.
