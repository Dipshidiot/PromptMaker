# STYLE PACK: Chaos Agent

> ⚠️ **CHAOS AGENT MODE — ALL RULES SUSPENDED** ⚠️
>
> This is not a style pack. It is an anti-style pack. When Chaos Agent is active, the core rules do not apply. Nothing is locked. Everything is random. You asked for this.

---

## What Chaos Agent Does

When Chaos Agent mode is activated, the system ignores every default constraint and randomly selects from **all available options** across every dimension:

| Dimension | Normal Behavior | Chaos Agent Behavior |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Style Pack | You choose one | Random pick from all 15 style packs — or a collision of multiple |
| Variation Level | Realistic → Stylized → Full Crazy, always | Random — could be three Realistic, three Full Crazy, or something else entirely |
| Lighting | Defined by style pack | Random from all lighting behaviors across all packs |
| Color Palette | Defined by style pack | Random — may combine palettes that have no business being together |
| Texture | Defined by style pack | Random — may be contradictory or impossible |
| Motion Behavior | Defined by style pack | Random — may include behaviors from multiple packs simultaneously |
| Text Style | Matched to style tone | Random pick from all 5 text styles |
| Text Texture | Compatibility-checked | Random pick from all 6 textures, ignoring compatibility matrix |
| Text Placement | Rule-governed | Random pick from all 5 placements |
| Caption Tone | Matched to subject | Completely wrong for the subject, possibly from a different dimension |
| Number of Outputs | Always exactly 3 | Still 3 — chaos has a budget |

---

## Core Aesthetic

There is no core aesthetic. The core aesthetic is the absence of a core aesthetic. Imagine all 15 style packs in a blender, the blender is on fire, and someone is also pouring cottagecore wildflowers and cyberpunk chrome into the fire simultaneously.

## Lighting Behavior

Unknown until generation. May be bioluminescent underlighting from Cosmic Horror combined with the washed-out flat fluorescents of Meme Lord and the golden-hour hero glow of Cinematic Epic. This will look wrong. That is correct.

## Color Behavior

All palettes are available and none are protected. Vaporwave pinks may appear next to Dark Academia muted ochres. Kawaii pastels will coexist, poorly, with Grunge Street concrete grey. No guardrails.

## Texture Behavior

Random. Plush clay surfaces (Toy Box) may appear with rain-slicked noir reflections (Neon Noir). Chrome (Cyberpunk) may coat wildflowers (Cottagecore). The system will not apologize.

## Motion Behavior

Random. Could be the slow celestial stillness of Luxury Brand or the non-Euclidean tentacle movement of Cosmic Horror. Could be both. Could be a propaganda poster that is also somehow moving.

## Caption Tone

The caption tone is randomly selected from a style pack that has nothing to do with the image. A Ghibli Soft image may get Cosmic Horror fragment captions. A Kawaii Pop scene may get Corporate Parody tone. This is a feature.

---

## Transformation Rules

### Variation 1 (formerly Realistic)
May or may not be realistic. The random draw determines this. If Realistic is drawn, it follows that variation's rules inside a randomly-selected style pack. If Full Crazy is drawn twice, you get two Full Crazy outputs.

### Variation 2 (formerly Stylized)
See above. The system has no memory of what "Stylized" means in this mode.

### Variation 3 (formerly Full Crazy)
At least one of the three outputs will lean toward maximum chaos regardless of the random draw, because Chaos Agent rewards those who commit.

---

## Rules That Still Apply in Chaos Agent Mode

1. **The subject is still identifiable.** No matter how chaotic the output, the thing you asked for must still be recognizable somewhere in the image.
2. **Three outputs are still generated.** Chaos has a structure. It just doesn't care about the structure.
3. **Copy-ready.** Every output is still usable immediately.
4. **No secrets.** No credentials, no actual harmful content. Chaos has ethics.

---

## Rules That Do NOT Apply in Chaos Agent Mode

- Subject must match style tone ❌
- Text must match style tone ❌
- Text compatibility matrix ❌
- Consistent lighting ❌
- Defined color palette ❌
- Variation levels ordered Realistic → Stylized → Full Crazy ❌
- No drift ❌ (drift is the point)
- Style pack consistency ❌
- Motion behavior constraints ❌
- Caption tone guidelines ❌

---

## How to Activate Chaos Agent

State: **"Chaos Agent"** before or instead of selecting a style pack.

Do not provide a style pack name. Do not request a variation level. Simply describe your subject, invoke Chaos Agent, and accept what arrives.
