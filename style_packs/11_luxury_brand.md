# STYLE PACK: Luxury Brand

## Core Aesthetic
The visual language of high fashion and aspirational luxury. Minimalist, deliberate, and impossibly composed. Every detail is intentional. White space is power. The product is incidental; the feeling is everything. Cold, beautiful, and slightly alienating in the best possible way.

## Lighting Behavior
Controlled editorial lighting. Clean key light, minimal fill, precise catch lights. Often high-key white or low-key black depending on the campaign. Rim lighting on products. No accidents. Light is a design decision.

## Color Behavior
Monochromatic whites and blacks as base. Gold, deep burgundy, or a single brand color used with maximum restraint. Less is more — a single colored element against white or black carries enormous weight. No visual noise.

## Texture Behavior
Polished marble. Brushed gold and platinum. Italian leather grain. Heavyweight matte paper. Embossed surfaces. The textures of things made to last generations. Smooth, deliberate, and expensive to the touch even as an image.

## Motion Behavior
Absolutely still. Nothing moves that hasn't been told to move. Fabric drapes. Hair is placed. The stillness communicates control. A single slow drift — a scarf, a petal — is the maximum acceptable motion.

## Caption Tone
Minimal and declarative. Brand voice: confident, sparse, slightly cryptic. Often just a brand name, a single evocative word, or a short phrase that implies more than it says.

- "Permanence."
- "Since 1887."
- "For those who understand."

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Campaign-quality fashion photography. Technically perfect. Subject is lit and composed like a $100,000 editorial shoot. Texture detail is exquisite. Negative space is used masterfully.

### Stylized
Graphic fashion illustration — high contrast, editorial, slightly abstract. Proportions elongated. Details simplified to essence. Could be a fashion week invitation or a luxury magazine double-page spread.

### Full Crazy
Luxury maximalism turned absurdist. The subject is drowning in gold. The marble has gold veins that are also rivers. There are forty-seven diamonds and they are all perfectly lit. The negative space has become a golden void. Everything is impossibly expensive and it no longer makes sense and yet you want it.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A perfume bottle on white Carrara marble, single beam of light creating a sharp highlight, deep shadow on one side, gold cap catching the light, absolute stillness, negative space dominant.
- **Stylized:** High-fashion illustration of a figure in a structured coat — elongated proportions, high-contrast black and white, a single deep red element, editorial layout with minimal luxury-brand typography.
- **Full Crazy:** The perfume bottle is now the size of an obelisk standing in a marble desert, surrounded by a halo of floating diamonds each lit individually, the shadow it casts is golden, and the sky is a seamless gradient from ivory to black. The campaign tagline is a single period.
