# STYLE PACK: Neon Noir

## Core Aesthetic
Rain-slicked streets, buzzing neon signs, and deep shadows. A marriage of classic 1940s film noir atmosphere with electric cyberpunk color. Melancholy, beautiful, and dangerous. Every scene feels like the opening shot of a detective story set 50 years in the future.

## Lighting Behavior
High-contrast chiaroscuro. Single dominant neon light source — pink, cyan, or amber — casting hard colored shadows. Secondary fill from distant city glow. Wet surfaces create reflective highlights. Deep blacks swallow everything not directly lit.

## Color Behavior
Palette of cyan, magenta, amber, and deep navy against near-black. Neon colors are fully saturated; shadows are almost desaturated. The contrast between the two creates dramatic tension. No soft transitions — light meets dark abruptly.

## Texture Behavior
Wet pavement grain. Subtle film grain overlay across the whole image. Rain droplets on surfaces or lens. Neon signs have a slight bloom and flicker texture. Edges are slightly soft from atmospheric haze.

## Motion Behavior
Rain streaks downward in slow vertical lines. Neon reflections shimmer on wet ground. Subjects are still — contemplative, watchful. Background may have a single moving element: a passing car, a flicker.

## Caption Tone
Noir voice-over style. Short, moody, first-person. Poetic but cold. Sentences trail off or hit like a punch.

- "She walked in like trouble always does. Quietly."
- "The city never sleeps. Neither do I. Not anymore."

## Transformation Rules

### Realistic
Photographic noir. Real city environment, real rain, practical neon sources. Subject is sharp; environment is moody. Think cinematic street photography. Color graded — pushed blues, pulled highlights.

### Stylized
Illustrated or painterly. Neon colors intensified to near-abstract. Rain becomes stylized streaks. Shadows are graphic and deliberate. Feels like a graphic novel panel in motion.

### Full Crazy
Neon overdrive. Every surface glows. Rain is fluorescent. Shadows have colors. The subject is silhouetted against an impossible aurora of clashing neon. Reality bends — walls drip light.

## Example Outputs
- **Realistic:** A lone figure in a trench coat standing at a rain-soaked intersection, cyan neon from a bar sign illuminating half their face, deep noir shadows, film grain.
- **Stylized:** Same figure as a graphic novel illustration — bold ink lines, magenta and teal palette, rain rendered as stylized diagonal streaks, moody caption in thin serif font.
- **Full Crazy:** The figure dissolves into a silhouette of pure cyan light, surrounded by impossible overlapping neon signs in every language, rain falling upward in electric pink, the street itself glowing like a circuit board.
